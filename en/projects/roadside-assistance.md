# Roadside assistance

**PHP · live**

Brochure site for a 24/7 towing and roadside assistance company: services,
gallery, company information and contact.

## What actually matters here

The visitor is most likely **stranded and on their phone.** The site was built
on that assumption:

- The phone number is visible on every page without scrolling
- Direct call and WhatsApp links — one tap, no copy-pasting a number
- Fast on mobile is the priority; no heavy images or libraries

On a corporate site "about us" can sit at the top. Here the only thing that
matters is **how many seconds it takes to reach the number.**

## Structure

Content and contact details come from a single configuration file, so a changed
number updates everywhere at once.
